Artisan Lineage

A Commitment to Precision

Chronovexa was established with a singular, unwavering mission: to rescue the fading art of true Swiss gear calibration and introduce it to luxury wristwatches. In a world increasingly saturated by digital screens and smart devices, we dedicate our studio to mechanical tourbillon movements and solid titanium casings. We believe a watch is not simply a time tool; it is a structural representation of personal taste, history, and aesthetic values.

Each movement gear is sourced individually from high-purity alloy mills in Geneva. Once received at our atelier, the balance springs are inspected, hand-coiled, and regulated entirely by hand. Hand-regulation removes gravity deviations without bulk weight, allowing gears to turn seamlessly. It is this minute attention to microscopic details that allows our products to sustain a Lifetime Repair Guarantee.

Our Three Horology Pillars

01. Hand Calibration

We partner exclusively with watchmakers who utilize manual escapement regulation systems, avoiding rapid machine setups completely.

02. Lifetime Servicing

Traditional Swiss movements are made of modular components. If a gear wears down, our horologists can replace it individually to restore function.

03. Bespoke Metallurgy

We mill our bezels, crowns, and case backs in solid grade-5 titanium, hand-polishing and vacuum-coating in platinum to prevent wear.

The Atelier Future

As Chronovexa looks to the upcoming decade, we remain anchored in our slow-craft principles. We maintain a small studio of certified horologists, limiting our global output to a few hundred curated timepieces per year. This exclusivity ensures that every watch shipped is personally inspected and hand-calibrated. Chronovexa is a lifetime commitment to design excellence, Swiss precision, and horological preservation.
